Although big brown bats were common, I worked with Eastern reds the most

They are small, about the size of a small fist

They have an orangish-red color. They mostly feed on flying insects.

Eastern Red Bat Facts

  1. Color: Red
  2. Size: Small, only weighing about .35 oz
  3. Roost: Solitarily in trees

Roosting habits

Most Eastern red bats roost solitarily, sometimes sharing a tree with only a few other bats. They often migrate south in the autumn, and so the summer is the best time to study them in Illinois. We were able to sample a few during the summer, very cute guys.
